| Directory | Use |
|---|---|
| `contracts/` | One schema-valid payload per contract type (see its README) |

| `case2/` … `case7/` | Oracle cases 2-7 (printed fin can, 4-motor cluster with elliptical fins, tube fins, dual deploy, three stages with freeform fins, two-stage dual deploy) generated by `python3 -I scripts/fixtures/oracle_case.py` (`--check` to verify) from `tests/oracle/case<n>.geometry.json` and `case<n>-generic.json` only (M2.1-M2.3). Each is the golden configuration and sim; the conditions hash equals the golden's (`crates/rw-api/tests/fixtures.rs`). Fin cross-sections come from the geometry's `cross_section_*` fields, and case 5's stage mass override (`stage_mass_override_kg`, including subcomponents) is `[stage.sustainer] override = { mass, subtree = true }` (M2.4c). Inputs the geometry does not carry: an inner-tube liner when a body tube is the motor mount (case 4), and zero-thickness shoulders left out. They keep the oracle's own layout, so `rw check` reports the couplers and bulkheads that overlap their tubes and motors longer than their mounts (cases 2, 5, 6, 7); they are parity inputs, not examples, and are not in the strict-check table below |
| `simple/` | A small three-fin rocket with a part include, params, a variant, and two configurations (M1.54c) |
| `cfd/` | RW-generated synthetic tank field in the Imported CFD v1 CSV format (R6.15): three steps `tank_t<s>.csv` with velocity and `p`, `T`, `alpha` scalars; see `cfd/PROVENANCE.md` (M5.4) |
| `research-vehicle/` | The concept-art vehicle (`concepts.md` §4.1) as a real model: four fins, appearance presets, a twist-lock recovery bay, configurations Baseline / Higher altitude / Recovery test, and simulations including Fin revision (M1.54c). It flies the 18 mm generic motors, so it is smaller than the art |

the checks listed in `templates/README.md`.
