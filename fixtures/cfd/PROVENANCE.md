# fixtures/cfd provenance

`tank_t0.0.csv`, `tank_t0.5.csv`, `tank_t1.0.csv` are a **synthetic field
generated by RocketWright**, not the output of a CFD solver and not taken from
any third-party data set. They exist to exercise the Imported CFD v1 format
(spec R6.15) and page 06's Imported CFD tab (Acc 9). They carry no
engineering meaning.

- Generator: `rw_project::cfd::synthetic_step(t)` in
  `crates/rw-project/src/cfd.rs` (closed-form expressions only).
- Regenerate: `RW_REGEN_CFD=1 cargo test -p rw-project --lib synthetic_fixture`.
  The same test fails when the committed files differ from the generator.
- Geometry: a horizontal cylinder in the body frame (+X aft from the nose tip,
  +Z up), x 0.62..1.02 m, radius 48 mm; 21 axial stations by 49 points
  (centre plus rings of 8, 16, and 24 at r/R = 1/3, 2/3, 1), 1029 points
  per step.
- Fields: a tilted free surface under a 30 m/s² aft specific force that moves
  aft as the tank drains and sloshes in pitch; `alpha` is a tanh step across
  it (liquid fraction, 1), `p` is ullage pressure plus ρ·a·depth with
  ρ = 790 kg/m³ (Pa), `T` is a smooth temperature field (K), and `vx,vy,vz`
  are a parabolic drain profile plus a slosh term (m/s).
- Metadata: each file has `# rw-cfd: units=p:Pa,T:K,alpha:1; time=<s>`, and
  the `_t<s>` name agrees with it.
